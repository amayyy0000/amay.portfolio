Subject Overview

Probability measures the chance of events happening in uncertain situations.

Statistics organizes, analyzes, and interprets data to help make informed decisions.

Applications

Used in engineering, data science, machine learning, finance, quality control, and scientific experiments.

What is Probability & Statistics?

Probability predicts uncertain outcomes, while Statistics explains patterns in data. Combined, they enable analytical thinking and evidence-based decisions.
